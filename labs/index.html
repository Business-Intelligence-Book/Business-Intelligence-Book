Business Intelligence and Analytics

Python Labs

Ten hands-on labs that follow the chapters of the book. Each lab has a guided walkthrough and exercises, and runs in the browser with Google Colab. For readers, and for instructors who build a course on the book.

The booksLabsHow it worksData

How each lab works

1. Walkthrough

A guided notebook shows the method step by step on a real dataset. It opens with what you will learn.

2. Exercises

A notebook with gaps to fill in. Checkpoint questions mark what to get right before moving on.

3. Run it

Click Open in Colab, then choose File, Save a copy in Drive to keep your changes. Nothing needs to be installed.
